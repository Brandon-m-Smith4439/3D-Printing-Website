# v0.99 — Production Search & Profit History

## Production workflow
- Adds search directly to the Production page.
- Search covers request code, customer, email, phone, project/description, owner notes, material/color, and linked queue code/title.
- Searching looks across all request statuses so older or completed work can be found without changing filters first.

## Dashboard actions
- Needs Attention is now an owner action queue instead of a general watch list.
- Only urgent or action-required items appear there; passive watch items no longer clutter the section.
- Adds Cost vs Profit to the main Dashboard:
  - active/quoted revenue
  - current direct cost
  - projected contribution profit
  - projected margin
  - all-time completed revenue
  - all-time completed direct cost
  - all-time completed profit and margin

## Historical custom requests
- Owner-created requests can be marked Historical / already completed.
- Historical entries record:
  - completion date
  - amount charged
  - total direct cost
  - calculated profit
  - calculated contribution margin
- Historical entries are created as completed bookkeeping records and do not send customer notifications or enter the quote/deposit/production workflow.
- Historical records appear in Production search and contribute to completed profitability.
- Pricing & Profitability now supports 7d, 30d, 90d, and All time ranges.

## Payment labels
- When Local pickup is selected, manual payment options display simply as Cash, Zelle, Cash App, Apple Cash, Venmo, and PayPal.
- When Carrier shipping is selected, those same options remain visible but disabled and labeled (Local pickup only).

## Compatibility
- Normal public custom requests remain unchanged apart from contextual payment labels.
- Existing v0.98 requests, quotes, payments, queue records, shipping, customer notifications, and security behavior remain intact.
- Historical profit records are stored as their own SQLite collection on the persistent Railway volume.
