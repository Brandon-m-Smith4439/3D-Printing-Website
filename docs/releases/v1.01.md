# v1.01 — Owner Custom Request polish and starting status

## Owner Custom Request
- Restyles the dedicated Owner Custom Request workspace to match Mesh Harbor 3D's dark marine glass-panel design.
- Groups the form into clear workflow, customer/project, fulfillment/job, print-detail, historical-profit, and notes sections.
- Adds a live status badge and workflow strip at the top of the workspace.
- Adds a **Current request status** selector for live owner-created requests:
  - New — awaiting review
  - Reviewing — scoping / planning
- Historical requests continue to be stored as completed records and display **Completed history**.

## Workflow integrity
- Quote Sent, Deposit Paid, Queue, Production, Ready, and Completed are intentionally not manually selectable during creation.
- Those later states remain driven by their linked quote, payment, queue, and completion records so the Production board does not show a stage that did not actually happen.

## Responsive polish
- The section cards and field grid collapse to one column on mobile.
- The workflow strip reflows at 720px and again at 430px.
- Sticky header and submit actions remain available while scrolling.
