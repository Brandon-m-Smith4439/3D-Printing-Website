# Owner Action Center

Sign in at https://meshharbor3d.com/owner, finish the authenticator step, then
select **Open AI Business Control Center**. The owner checklist appears near
the top of https://meshharbor3d.com/owner/business.

1. Open an item under **Approvals**. Read the exact brief and spending ceiling
   or completed draft. Approve or reject it in the existing inbox. Draft approval
   is not publication approval. Four historical leader calls currently await
   spending approval; avoid approving obsolete duplicate plans.
2. Open a **Products** item to select its project. Expand **Record physical test
   of the latest revision** and record the actual current file/material result.
   Expand **Prepare product for sale** to review real costs, channel fees,
   comparable asking prices and the margin floor. Market signals are not sales.
3. Pair the local slicing computer using [the setup guide](local-slicing-worker.md).
   Check your printer, nozzle and plate match the verified profile. Enter real
   finished-part weight, box/protection weights, dimensions and packing costs.
   Confirm multicolor hardware and model assignments before multicolor slicing.
4. Verify seller tax settings, destinations, customer shipping charges,
   fulfillment/return policies, separate website/Etsy stock and actual product
   presentation. These owner decisions cannot be inferred from an API key.
5. The website currently uses Stripe and EasyPost test configurations. Set up
   production billing/keys and matching signed webhooks privately and approve
   enabling live commerce separately. Never use a TEST label as postage.
6. Approve the exact tested release and website catalog snapshot. Etsy publishing
   additionally needs a separate listing-write grant and exact listing/fee review.
   Read-only Etsy access alone cannot publish. Do not launch an empty untested catalog.
7. For paid physical orders, **Shipping** opens the exact record. Confirm actual
   packing, get rates, approve one price, print the private 4×6 label and confirm
   carrier handoff. Unknown purchase outcomes must be reconciled before retrying.

The checklist clears items from recorded results, not a cosmetic “done” checkbox.
Setup failures explicitly show an incomplete-check warning. Task/project data
refreshes every ten seconds; setup/orders every thirty seconds while visible.
All pending task approvals are included. Failure history covers the latest 200
tasks; shipping covers the latest 100 physical catalog orders. Custom
quotes and in-person projects remain on the owner operations board.

Configuration checks do not establish a successful live transaction. The wider
web research collector and actual multicolor slicer remain additional implementation
and validation work. No unattended public listing, marketing send or purchase is
enabled by this checklist. The website being online and product sales being
enabled are separate states.
